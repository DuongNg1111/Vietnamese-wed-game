🎴 Vietnamese Directions Memory Match

📖 How to Play

1. Click one numbered card.
2. Click another numbered card.
3. Match the Vietnamese word with its English meaning.
4. Correct pairs stay open.
5. Wrong pairs flip back.
6. Match all pairs to move to the next round.

Correct Match

bên trái ✅ Left
