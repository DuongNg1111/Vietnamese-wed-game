How Much / How Many — Bao nhiêu / Mấy?

"Bao nhiêu / Mấy" /bao nyew / may/ are question words for asking how much or how many.

Pattern

subject + verb + bao nhiêu / mấy + unit + noun

Mấy /may/ is commonly used for small numbers, especially under 10.
Bao nhiêu /bao nyew/ can be used for larger or unspecified quantities.

Examples

Bạn mua bao nhiêu chai nước?
ban mua bao nyew chai nook?
How many bottles of water do you buy?
Bạn uống mấy ly cà phê?
ban uong may lee ka feh?
How many cups of coffee do you drink?
Bạn có mấy anh chị em?
ban kaw may an chee em?
How many siblings do you have?
