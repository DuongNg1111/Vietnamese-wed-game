WHAT — CÁI GÌ?

"Cái gì /kai zee/ " is the question word for the What-question.

PATTERN

subject + verb + CÁI GÌ?

cái gì /kai zee/ are question words used to ask what something is.

EXAMPLES

Bạn ăn cái gì?
ban an kai zee?
What do you eat?
Bạn uống cái gì?
ban uong kai zee?
What do you drink?
Bạn thích cái gì?
ban thik kai zee?
What do you like?
