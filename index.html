About this website

Vietnamese practice, made simple.

Hi, I'm Dương, a Vietnamese tutor on Preply .

I first created these games to help my students practice between lessons and remember what they learned.

Now, I'm making them available to anyone learning Vietnamese — whether you're my student, just getting started, planning a trip, or simply looking for a few minutes of practice each day.

This site focuses on short, practical vocabulary games for absolute beginners (A0–A1). No lectures, no long lessons — just simple activities to help you learn and review useful Vietnamese words.

Vietnamese is more approachable than it looks. Start with a few useful words, practice them often, and build your vocabulary step by step. A little practice goes a long way.
